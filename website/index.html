Three ways to connect

Search however the moment calls for it

WAY doesn't lock you into one way of finding people. Pick the mode that fits.

Nearby

A live radar shows distance and direction to people around you right now, filtered by category or interest — toggle to list or grid view anytime.

Address

Type any street address on the planet and search within a radius around it — built for the moment you need eyes somewhere specific.

Location

Drop a pin on a map, or just type a city, state, or country — find connections anywhere in the world, no GPS required.

Everything in one app

Built around real connection

From a timeline that feels like home to a wallet that moves with you — WAY is the whole toolkit.

Timeline & Feed

Post photos, videos, and status updates. Like, comment, share, and track views — with an auto-scroll WAY Button for hands-free browsing.

Multi-currency Wallet

Fund with a card, send money to any WAY instantly, and keep a transaction history — general, or filtered to one specific person.

Going Live

Broadcast to your followers, schedule ahead, and chat live — with a push notification the moment you go on.

Remote Camera Access

Ask someone, anywhere, to point their camera at what's happening — nothing streams until they explicitly say yes.

Multiple Profiles

Two free profiles per account — keep a personal and a business identity running independently under one login.

Visibility, Your Way
